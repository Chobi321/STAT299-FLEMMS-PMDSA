Here are my major comments:
1. Remove references on the Geographic Weighted Regression (GWR) and the Family Income
and Expenditure Survey (FIES) to align the manuscript with the presentation. The objectives
should also be adjusted, de-emphasizing the angle on spatial inference and then further
emphasizing the predictive and policy analysis components.
2. Explain further the Average Local Effects (ALE), and how exactly it makes the ML models
more interpretable. Perhaps they can compare and contrast with marginal effects, which is
how we traditionally study the effect of one independent variable on an outcome variable
in a linear regression framework, ceteris paribus.
3. For the Principal Component Analysis (PCA) method, you should develop this further in the
manuscript. Pin down exactly what you use it for. It seems to me that it is being deployed to
conduct dimension reduction, feature selection, as well as a bit of policy analysis (in
interpreting the components of the top ten PCs).
4. On the Friedman H-statistic, I’ll be interested how your analysis will change (if at all) if their
values are closer to 1, suggesting interaction effects.
5. I second Sir John Eustaquio’s comments on: (i) cleaning up the imputation methodology,
and (ii) pinning down the role of PCA in the paper — would it be for pure prediction or
policy analysis?
