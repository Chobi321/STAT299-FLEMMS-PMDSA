# 02. Literature Review & Definition Shifts

- **Functional Literacy Framework:** UNESCO (2020) basis. Shift from the old definition ("automatic qualification" via a high school credential) to the post-2024 benchmark requiring direct proof of reading, writing, computing, *and comprehension*.
- **Identified Literature Gaps:**
  - *Spatial Inequality Gap:* Prior spatial work (Owada et al., 2019) used old 2008 FLEMMS data and limited its scope only to school-aged children, ignoring the broader working-age block.
  - *Methodological Gap:* Existing local literature relies on descriptive mapping rather than inferential spatial econometrics (Moran's I/LISA) or predictive supervised classification.
  - *Aspatial ML Bias:* General XGBoost models assume data is independent and identically distributed, ignoring spatial non-stationarity (e.g., internet access behaves as a baseline requirement in HUCs but is a high-impact differentiator in rural regions).
