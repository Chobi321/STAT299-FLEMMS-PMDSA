# 01. Introduction, Objectives, & Scope

- **Title:** A Spatial and Machine Learning Approach to Identifying High-Risk Functional Literacy Areas in the Philippines
- **Problem Statement:** Broad national metrics mask localized literacy vulnerabilities. 2024 FLEMMS shows 70.8% functional literacy (comprehension-focused) vs ~90% basic literacy, creating a 19-million citizen "labor force lag". Driven by a socio-technical ecosystem: the digital divide (e.g., San Juan City at 94.5% vs remote provinces) and intergenerational learning cycles.
- **Objectives:**
  1. Evaluate spatial inequality of functional literacy among working-age populations across provinces and Highly Urbanized Cities (HUCs).
  2. Quantify socioeconomic, demographic, and spatial drivers (internet, income dynamics, infrastructure).
  3. Develop a Gradient Boosting-driven Early Warning System (EWS) to simulate policy scenarios.
- **Scope & Limitations:**
  - Population aged 10 to 64 years old (FLEMMS Forms 2 and 3). 
  - National sample size: ~496,770 observations with applied survey weights.
  - Proxy constraints: No direct household income data; utilizes housing materials and asset ownership as socioeconomic proxies.
